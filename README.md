## Bambulab P2S Useful stuffs
Personal collection of mods that I find and test/use on makerworld. Purely using this as a way to keep track of useful stuffs for anyone who is looking for upgrades, testing/calibration things.

## Outer printer mods
- [Dot Dampers P2S Anti-Wobble feet](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2613271-dot-dampers-p2s-anti-wobble-feet-low-profile#profileId-2884010) - Removes most of the worrying wobble issues that the P2S has.

## AMS Mods
- [AMS 2 Desiccant Box (no Spool Removal required)](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1343840-ams-2-desiccant-box-no-spool-removal-required#profileId-1686176) - A must-have if you have AMS 2 Pro, helps reduce humdity levels it is best to paired it with ISOLATECH Reusable Silica Gel Bags.
- [AMS Riser 2.0 ](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1455867-ams-riser-2-0-for-p1s-p2s-x1c-x2d#profileId-1517346) - Allows the AMS to be raised for easier top glass access and also adds a spool holder in the back.

## Chamber upgrades
- [P2S Aux fan diffuser](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1926190-p2s-and-x2d-auxiliary-fan-diffuser#profileId-2134733) - Redirect airflow towards the top area while also optimizing airlow in the chamber.
- [Anti-grinding ptfe](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2288882-x1c-p1s-p2s-x2d-ptfe-anti-glass-grinding-mod#profileId-2497185) - Avoid the annoying grind that happens.
- [Dust Cover for the Inner Rear Panel](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2292308-bambu-lab-p2s-x1c-back-hole-dust-cover-with-chute?from=search#profileId-2501309) - Dust cover panel for inner real panel.

## Calibration / Testing stuffs
- [z-offset test](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2581022-p2s-first-layer-corner-test?from=search#profileId-2846646) - Great way to test first layer and see what works and what does not.

## Cleaning / Maintenance
- [PEI Textured plate cleaning](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/filament-acc/acc/pei-plate-clean-guide) - Bambulab Clean guide for Texture PEI plate.
